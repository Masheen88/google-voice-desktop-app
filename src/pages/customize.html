Floating mini call controller

Keep call controls available above other windows, including while Voice Desktop is minimized.

The 1 and 2 buttons click Google Voice's native in-call keypad without leaving it open. On screened incoming calls, click Answer first. When Google's keypad can't be detected, use the full Google Voice window (Ctrl+Shift+M).

GIF picker

Search animated GIFs inside your Google Voice conversations.

Create a free API key at developers.giphy.com. It stays on your computer and is used by the local GIF picker. Clearing this field and saving removes it.

Selected GIFs attach through Google Voice's normal image workflow. The app never sends your message automatically. GIFs must be no larger than 2 MB.
